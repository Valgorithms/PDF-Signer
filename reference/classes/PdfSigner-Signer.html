Signer.php : 32

Adds signature images, and marks such as ticks and lines, on top of the pages of an existing PDF.

The PDF is revised with an incremental update: its bytes are kept as they are and the signatures are appended, so everything it held survives, including any earlier digital signature as a prior revision.

Signer::open('lease.pdf')
    ->stamp(SignatureImage::fromFile('signature.png'), page: 3, x: 72, y: 640, width: 160)
    ->save('lease-signed.pdf');

This places a visible signature. It is not a cryptographic digital signature.
