Mark.php : 20

A mark drawn on a page as a vector path: a tick, a cross, a dot, a line, a rectangle or an ellipse. For ticking a box, striking something out or drawing a box around it on a form.

$signer->mark(new Mark(Mark::TICK), page: 1, x: 72, y: 300, width: 14, height: 14);

It fills a rectangle on the page the way a signature does; a line runs corner to corner, so a flat rectangle makes a straight line.
